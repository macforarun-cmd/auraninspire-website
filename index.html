ABOUT AURANINSPIRE

What AuranInspire is, and who it's for

Aviation Solutions

Corporate & Consulting

Aviation-oriented consulting and solutions for airlines, airports, and industry partners — bringing practical, real-world aviation expertise into corporate engagements.

Education

Aviation Education & Training

Structured, practical aviation education — airline operations, airport management, cabin crew training, and leadership development — built by an aviation educator.

Innovation

Research & Innovation

Forward-looking research and innovation work — sustainability, digital aviation, and emerging technology shaping the future of the industry.

What we do

AuranInspire brings together aviation solutions, education, and innovation under one organization — built to close the gap between classroom aviation theory and real industry practice.

Who it's for

Students building a career in airline, airport, or aviation management; industry partners looking for aviation consulting and solutions; and professionals seeking structured, practical training.

FOUNDER & AVIATION EDUCATOR

Arun Kumar

With years of academic and industry experience across aviation management education, Arun Kumar founded AuranInspire to close the gap between classroom aviation theory and real industry practice — combining teaching, research, and innovation under one roof.
